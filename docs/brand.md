# CodeMtaani Group Brand Foundation

## Identity

**Name:** CodeMtaani Group  
**Slogan:** Think. Build. Transform.

## Positioning

A Kenyan-rooted, Africa-focused and globally ambitious technology group developing digital products, delivering technology services, advancing research and innovation, and creating opportunities through technology.

## Brand principles

- Credible and professional.
- Contemporary and technology-led.
- Clear, confident and practical.
- Inclusive and internationally legible.
- Rooted in African opportunity without limiting global ambition.

## Initial visual direction

- Deep forest / near-black green for depth and trust.
- Bright electric green for energy and emphasis.
- Warm off-white and muted sage for readable content surfaces.
- Strong typography, generous spacing and restrained motion.
- Avoid generic stock-tech imagery and visual clichés.

## Voice

Use direct, precise language. Explain what the organisation does, who it serves and what outcomes it seeks. Avoid inflated claims, unverified client lists, invented metrics and claims of regulatory accreditation.
